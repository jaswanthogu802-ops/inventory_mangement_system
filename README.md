# 📦 StockPro – Inventory Management System

## 📌 About the Project

StockPro is a web-based Inventory Management System developed to help small shop owners and retail businesses manage their products, monitor stock availability, record sales, and generate sales reports in an easy and organized way.

Many small business owners manage their inventory manually, which can make it difficult to track available products, identify low-stock items, calculate sales, and maintain records. StockPro aims to simplify these activities through a user-friendly web application.

The application provides a centralized dashboard where users can view important inventory statistics, manage products, monitor stock levels, and access sales information. It is designed with a simple interface so that users with limited technical knowledge can understand and use the system comfortably.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- To simplify product and inventory management.
- To maintain product details such as name, category, price, and quantity.
- To identify low-stock and out-of-stock products.
- To record sales transactions and update stock quantities automatically.
- To maintain sales history for reviewing previous transactions.
- To generate sales reports for different time periods.
- To provide dashboard statistics and charts for better understanding of inventory and sales.
- To create a simple and user-friendly application for small businesses.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Dashboard
Displays important inventory statistics, including total products, stock levels, low-stock products, and sales information. Charts help users understand inventory and sales data visually.

### 2. Product Management
Allows users to add new products, edit existing product details, search for products, and delete products when necessary.

### 3. Stock Management
Tracks product quantities and identifies low-stock and out-of-stock items. Stock quantities are updated when products are sold.

### 4. Sales Management
Allows users to select available products, enter quantities, calculate the total sale amount, and update the remaining stock automatically.

### 5. Sales History
Maintains records of completed sales, including product names, quantities, prices, total amounts, dates, and times.

### 6. Sales Reports
Generates sales summaries for selected periods, such as today, this week, this month, or all recorded sales. Reports include transaction details and can be exported as CSV files.

### 7. Local Data Storage
Uses browser LocalStorage to retain product and sales records between page visits in the same browser.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5:** Structures the web pages and application sections.
- **CSS3:** Provides styling, layouts, and a user-friendly interface.
- **JavaScript:** Implements application logic, product management, sales calculations, and interactive features.
- **DOM Manipulation:** Updates dashboard statistics, product tables, and page content dynamically.
- **LocalStorage:** Stores inventory and sales data in the browser.
- **Chart.js:** Used for dashboard charts, if configured in the project.
